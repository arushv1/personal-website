# Posters

`vascupath-poster.webp` (4098 × 2304) and `vascupath-poster-preview.webp` (1600 × 900) are slide 1, the main poster, of `Vyas.Arush.Final.pptx`, supplied by Arush on November 10, 2026 (SHA-256 `b1eaaf001bdc91ab2582b3752d7be279938a36ca42c3489a80e9b35aa151f761`). He asked that only the first slide be used; the other 14 slides are not published here.

The slide was rendered at 100 dpi with LibreOffice and encoded as WebP. Nothing was redrawn or edited. LibreOffice substituted the metric-compatible Carlito and Liberation Sans for Calibri and Arial, so letterforms differ very slightly from PowerPoint. If an exact match matters, export slide 1 from PowerPoint as a PNG and replace both files, keeping the names and the 16:9 shape (update `width` and `height` in `src/content/portfolio.mjs` if the size changes).

The "+" and "›" buttons on the poster linked to the other slides in the original file. They are part of the image here and do nothing.

The poster appears under Publications as `vascupath-poster`. See `CONTENT.md` for the `poster` field.
